Find the gap behind a maths struggle

A map of Queensland maths from Year 9 (Australian Curriculum v9.0) to the QCAA 2025 senior subjects. Each topic links to the topics it depends on. The diagnosis asks a few questions and, when a student is stuck, works back through those prerequisites to find where the gap starts.

How it works

  1. Pick a target. One topic, a whole unit, or a year level.
  2. Answer up to 2 questions per topic. Right first time means mastered, so earlier topics are skipped.
  3. Wrong twice? The tool moves to that topic's prerequisites and keeps going back until it finds solid ground.
  4. Get a learning path from the root-cause gap up to the target, in teaching order.
